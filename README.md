# FrostRoute Cold Chain: Business App

**Business:** Cold Chain Logistics Company (ITBT Live Class Exercise)

## Problem
Hospitals and food businesses cannot tell whether their vaccines, medicines or frozen goods stayed at a safe temperature during delivery. A warm journey spoils cargo and the customer finds out too late.

## Solution
A web app that shows each shipment's status, location and live temperature, and gives the manager a dashboard of the numbers that matter.

## Customer
Hospitals, pharmacies, vaccine distributors, dairy units, restaurants and food retailers.

## Sections
1. **Home**: name, logo, purpose, "Get Started" button
2. **About**: what we do, customers, service area, value promise
3. **Services**: price table with enquiry links
4. **Dashboard**: 6 KPIs and a monthly orders bar chart
5. **Track shipment** (interactive feature): enter an Order ID (try `CC1025`, `CC1026`, `CC1027`)
6. **Contact**: email, phone, location, enquiry form

## Hyperlinks and their business purpose
| Link | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Get Started to Track shipment | Lets a customer check cargo straight away |
| Services "Enquire" to Contact | Turns interest in a price into a lead |
| Dashboard to Track shipment | Manager drills from KPIs to one shipment |
| Google Maps | Customers find the hub |
| Email and phone links | One-tap contact |
| LinkedIn | Partnerships and careers |
| FSSAI and NCCD | Shows compliance with food-safety and cold-chain standards |

## Dashboard: what the manager needs to know immediately
Total orders, delivered, in transit, **temperature excursions**, on-time delivery %, and reefer trucks available.
