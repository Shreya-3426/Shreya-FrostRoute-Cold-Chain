FrostRoute Cold Chain

Medicines and food that arrive as cold as they left.

Our purpose is to protect vaccines, medicines and fresh food from warm journeys. We combine refrigerated trucks, cold storage and live temperature tracking across Eastern India.

About the business

What we do

Temperature-controlled transport and storage, with a data logger in every consignment.

Target customers

Hospitals, pharmacies, vaccine distributors, dairy units, restaurants and food retailers.

Service area

Eastern India: Kolkata hub with daily routes to Jharkhand, Odisha, Bihar and Assam.

Our promise

If the temperature leaves the safe range, the customer is alerted within minutes, not after delivery.

Compliance: we follow food-safety rules from FSSAI and cold-chain guidance from the National Centre for Cold-chain Development.
